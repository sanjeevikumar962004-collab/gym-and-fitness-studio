Can I pause or freeze my membership if traveling?

Yes. Both Pro and Elite VIP tiers include complimentary membership pause privileges up to 60 days per calendar year. Simply trigger the pause via your Stackly Member Portal at least 48 hours prior.

payments

What payment methods are supported in Salem?

We accept UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), all major domestic & international Credit/Debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay, Amex), and automated monthly e-NACH mandates for seamless billing.

chevron_backward

Can I switch my dedicated personal trainer?

Absolutely. Trainer-athlete chemistry is critical for apex performance. If you wish to rotate to a coach specializing in powerlifting, Olympic weightlifting, or sports rehab, our desk handles switches instantly without penalty.

badge

How do guest passes work for Pro & Elite tiers?

Guest passes renew automatically every calendar month in your member app. Your guests receive full day-pass privileges when accompanied by you, including locker, sauna, and conditioning floor access.

schedule

Is the Salem Flagship genuinely open 24/7?

Yes. Pro and Elite members use encrypted NFC phone tap or dynamic QR codes for biometrically verified, secure entry through our MMR Complex turnstiles at any hour, 365 days a year.

How does the 14-Day Risk-Free guarantee operate?

If within your first 14 days you decide Stackly Fitness isn't the right training home for your goals, email concierge@stacklyfitness.in or speak with the MMR Complex desk for an immediate, no-questions-asked refund.
